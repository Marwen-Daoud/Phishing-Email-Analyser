Educational triage tool only. This tool looks for common warning signs. It cannot prove an email is safe or malicious, and a low score does not mean safe. Never click suspicious links or open attachments to "test" them. Verify anything important using the organisation's official website, app or a contact number you already know, never details from the email itself.
All demo emails are fictional and use reserved example domains. Scoring rules are listed in plain text in the file analyser.js (search for RULES), so you can read and change them.
